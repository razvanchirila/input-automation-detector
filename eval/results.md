## Caveats

- The corpus is synthetic. These numbers measure how well the detectors separate *this* generator's populations. They are not an estimate of real-world accuracy; that needs labelled capture from production.
- The evasive population encodes one specific set of humanizer assumptions. An adversary who also models minimum-jerk velocity profiles and overshoot-and-correct reaching would score lower. The dwell shape sub-signal is already near-useless against it.
- The combined score is a ranking statistic, not a calibrated probability. Its absolute value is not meaningful; only the ordering and the fitted threshold are.
- Sessions are short bursts of roughly 15-23 action units. Sub-signals that need sample size, notably dwell skewness, are noisy at this length and are weighted accordingly.
